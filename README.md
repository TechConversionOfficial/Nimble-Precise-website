## About the business

- **What it does:** Nimble Precise delivers structured, practice-led learning programmes in mental arithmetic, mathematical techniques, problem-solving and digital skills. It also promotes training routes for educators who want to teach these subjects.
- **Who it serves:** children and other learners, parents comparing enrichment programmes, tutors, teachers and potential education partners.
- **Who is behind it:** the live site names **Anita Tom** as Founder & Educator. Her public professional profile also identifies her with Nimble Precise and abacus education.
- **Why the website matters:** families need to see syllabus levels, activities and assessment methods before committing. Educators need a different explanation of training, curriculum and possible teaching opportunities.

## The problem

An education provider speaking to both parents and educators needs two clear journeys without making the website feel like two unrelated businesses. A parent looking for an Abacus course needs to understand the child's age group, level, classroom activities and progression. A teacher exploring certification needs to know the training focus and how to enquire.

Nimble Precise also offers several quite different subjects. If those programmes are presented as a single undifferentiated list, visitors can struggle to compare them or find the relevant syllabus. The website therefore needs to organise **the audience, the curriculum and the next step** in a way that works on a phone as well as a desktop.

These are the communication needs visible in the public site, not a documented audit of an earlier version.

## How Tech Conversion approached the website

The points below describe the **published site's approach**. They should not be read as a reconstruction of unverified internal development steps.

1. **Separate the two primary audiences.** The navigation and homepage offer distinct [student](https://nimbleprecise.com/for-students/) and [educator](https://nimbleprecise.com/for-educators/) journeys, so each visitor can start with the information that matters to them.
2. **Give each subject its own explanation.** Individual sections cover [Abacus](https://nimbleprecise.com/abacus/), [Vedic Maths](https://nimbleprecise.com/vedic-maths/), [Rubik's Cube](https://nimbleprecise.com/rubiks-cube/) and [Basics in Computers](https://nimbleprecise.com/basics-in-computers/), rather than hiding every programme inside a generic courses page.
3. **Show what is taught, level by level.** The Abacus page sets out progression from Acquirers through Zenith, with age-based exploration, curriculum topics, practice and assessment information.
4. **Explain learning through a simple sequence.** The homepage presents **Understand → Practise → Perform → Progress** to make the educational approach readable for parents unfamiliar with the teaching methods.
5. **Keep educator training visible.** Training and certification are given their own positioning, rather than being treated as a small footnote below student courses.
6. **Make progress and community part of the story.** The public website contains testimonials, events, learning-community information and a [results page](https://nimbleprecise.com/result/) presenting abacus competition achievements.
7. **Offer a direct next step.** Demo-class enquiries, course guidance and educator-partnership enquiries appear as calls to action. The website also offers a separate free-trial booking link.

## Standards the website should be checked against

These are **appropriate review references**, not an assertion of completed certification, testing or technical compliance.

- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): assess loading, interaction responsiveness and layout stability, especially for image-rich learning pages on mobile.
- [WCAG 2.2, Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): review keyboard use, focus visibility, contrast, headings and form labels for both children and adults.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): review discoverable course information, page indexing and useful descriptions of curricula.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): evaluate appropriate organisation, course and breadcrumb data where supported by accurate page content.
- [India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework](https://www.meity.gov.in/data-protection-framework): examine applicable consent and handling of parent, child, tutor and enrolment enquiries, paying special attention to children's information.
- **Education-claim accuracy:** verify statements about certification recognition, learning outcomes, student totals and testimonials against original records and authorisations.
